Data Shard

Right Now

In English, we use the PRESENT CONTINUOUS to talk about actions happening in this exact moment.

> Formula: Person + To Be (am/is/are) + Action + ING

"The girl is looking."

"The rain is falling."

"They are shooting."

Data Shard

Negative & Question

> Negative (NOT)

"She is not smiling."

> Question (Invert Am/Is/Are)

"Are they shooting?"

"Is it raining?"
